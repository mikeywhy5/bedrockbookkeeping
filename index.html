FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is bookkeeping for small businesses?

Bookkeeping is the foundation of a healthy business. It involves accurately recording, organizing, and maintaining your financial transactions—from income and expenses to invoices and payroll.

Whether you're just starting out or managing a growing business, reliable bookkeeping gives you a clear understanding of where your money is coming from, where it's going, and how your business is performing.

At BEDROCK BOOKKEEPING, we make the process simple and stress-free, giving you more time to focus on what matters most, running and growing your business.

Getting started is easy. Schedule a free consultation with us to discuss your business, your bookkeeping needs, and your goals. From there, we'll create a tailored bookkeeping solution designed to give you clarity and confidence in your finances.

Why hire Bedrock Bookkeeping instead of an in-house bookkeeper?

Hiring an employee costs more than just their salary. Employer-paid expenses—including CPP and EI contributions, vacation pay, paid sick days, and other benefits—can add significantly to the true cost of maintaining an in-house employee.

There are also the hidden costs of recruiting, training, performance management, and employee retention. When you hire in-house, you're also limited to one individual's experience, availability, and capacity.

With BEDROCK BOOKKEEPING, you get a dedicated bookkeeping professional focused on keeping your finances organized and accurate—without the added costs and responsibilities of hiring an employee.

You'll know exactly what your monthly bookkeeping costs are while receiving personalized, professional service tailored to your business. With clear and reliable financial information, you can make informed, data-driven decisions with greater confidence.

How much does bookkeeping cost for a small business?

The cost of bookkeeping for a small business can vary depending on your unique needs. Generally, you can expect to invest between $200 and $600 per month. Your final cost will depend on factors such as the number of monthly transactions, the complexity of your finances, and the specific services your business requires.

At lower price points, bookkeeping services typically include essential tasks such as transaction recording and monthly financial reporting. More comprehensive services may include payroll support, tax compliance assistance, financial analysis, and additional advisory services.

At BEDROCK BOOKKEEPING, we understand that no two businesses are alike. That's why we offer customized bookkeeping solutions tailored to your specific needs and budget. We'll work with you to build a plan that provides the right level of support—giving you the financial clarity and confidence to focus on growing your business.

Are bookkeeping services tax-deductible?

Absolutely! Professional bookkeeping services are generally considered a legitimate business expense and may be tax-deductible.

By investing in professional bookkeeping, you're not only keeping your finances accurate and organized, you may also reduce your taxable business income. It's an investment that provides valuable financial clarity while potentially offering tax benefits.

At BEDROCK BOOKKEEPING, we help you stay organized, informed, and focused on what matters most: growing your business.

What is the best bookkeeping software for small businesses?

Finding the right bookkeeping software depends on the unique needs of your business. Popular platforms like QuickBooks, Xero, and FreshBooks offer powerful features designed to help businesses manage their finances and scale as they grow.

At BEDROCK BOOKKEEPING, we specialize in QuickBooks and are certified QuickBooks experts. We prefer "QBO" for its comprehensive features, powerful integrations, and ability to provide a clear picture of your business finances.

While QuickBooks is an incredibly capable platform, its extensive tools and features can feel overwhelming when you're managing a business on your own. That's where we come in.

We'll handle the bookkeeping for you and help you get the most out of your QuickBooks account—so you can benefit from powerful financial tools without needing to become an expert yourself.
